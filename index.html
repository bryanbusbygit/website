About

Research interests include combinatorics, discrete dynamical systems, and RF propagation, with recent work on arithmetical structures on graphs, transient length bounds for Boolean monomial systems, and propagation measurements across microwave and millimeter-wave bands.

Research

Arithmetical Structures on Graphs

This project studies arithmetical structures on cycles with a multi-edge. The work focuses on the classification and enumeration of arithmetical structures, including constructive descriptions and counting formulas for the families that arise on these graphs. Advised by Alexander Díaz and Joel Louwsma.

Nonlinear Boolean Monomial Systems

This project studies transient length bounds for Boolean monomial systems. Using dependency graphs and acyclic subgraphs, the work derives upper bounds on transient length and relates those bounds to combinatorial properties of the underlying digraph, including number-theoretic arguments based on the Frobenius number. Advised by Omar Colón.

5G RF Propagation

This work uses SDR-based propagation measurements in microwave and millimeter-wave bands relevant to 5G systems. It includes GNU Radio instrumentation, Vivaldi antenna design, and channel measurements for studying attenuation and directional effects in real RF environments. Advised by Rafael Solís.
