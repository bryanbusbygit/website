About

I am an undergraduate at the University of Puerto Rico, with a particular interest in discrete dynamical systems and their connections to number theory. I keep this page to collect projects and share links.

Research

Arithmetical Structures on Graphs

Arithmetical structures on cycles with a multi-edge are studied here, with a focus on classification and enumeration, including constructive descriptions and counting formulas for the families that arise on these graphs. Advised by Alexander Díaz and Joel Louwsma.

Nonlinear Boolean Monomial Systems

This work studies transient length bounds for Boolean monomial systems using dependency graphs and acyclic subgraphs. The work derives upper bounds on transient length and relates those bounds to combinatorial properties of the underlying digraph, including number-theoretic arguments based on the Frobenius number. Advised by Omar Colón.

5G RF Propagation Measurements

SDR-based propagation measurements in microwave and millimeter-wave bands are used here for 5G-related RF studies, along with GNU Radio instrumentation, Vivaldi antenna design, and channel measurements for studying attenuation and directional effects in real RF environments. The work also includes debugging issues that arose in GNU Radio during measurement and signal-processing workflows. Advised by Rafael Solís.
