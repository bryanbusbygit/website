Research

Arithmetical structures on graphs. Combinatorics research advised by Alexander Díaz and Joel Louwsma. We worked on arithmetical structures on graphs, especially cycles with a multi-edge.

Nonlinear Boolean dynamical systems. Finite dynamical systems research advised by Omar Colón. We worked on transient bounds for Boolean monomial systems.

5G RF propagation / GNU Radio. RF measurements work advised by Rafael Solís. We developed a radio-wave propagation setup for 5G bands and used GNU Radio for the measurements.
