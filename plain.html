About

I’m Bryan C. Busby. My research is in combinatorics, discrete dynamical systems, and RF propagation.

My recent work includes arithmetical structures on graphs, transient length bounds for Boolean monomial systems, and propagation measurements from microwave to millimeter-wave bands.

Research

Selected projects in combinatorics, discrete dynamical systems, and RF propagation.

Arithmetical Structures on Graphs

This project studies arithmetical structures on cycles with a multi-edge. Advised by Alexander Diaz and Joel Louwsma.

Nonlinear Boolean Monomial Systems

This project studies transient length bounds for Boolean monomial systems through dependency graphs and acyclic subgraphs. Advised by Omar Colon.

5G RF Propagation

This work involves SDR-based propagation measurements, GNU Radio, and Vivaldi antenna design across microwave and millimeter-wave bands. Advised by Rafael Solis.
