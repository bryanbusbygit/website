Figure Library

Figures used on the research pages, with links to their sources.

Cycles with a multi-edge

Source material from Busby, Dogbatse, and Palma, manuscript in preparation. The notation \(\widetilde C_{n,k}\) means a cycle on \(n\geq3\) vertices with one edge replaced by \(k\geq2\) parallel edges.

Paths with a doubled edge

Source material from D. Glass and J. Wagner, “Arithmetical Structures on Paths With a Doubled Edge” (arXiv). Redrawn with hollow, labeled vertices under CC BY 4.0. The repository record gives the license statement.

Boolean monomial dynamics

Source material from Busby, “On Acyclic Subgraphs and Transient Length Bounds,” 2024 poster. For nonzero squarefree monomial coordinates, the dependency graph \(D_f\) has a directed edge \(j\to i\) if and only if \(x_j\mid f_i\).

Finite state spaces

Source material from R. Laubenbacher and B. Pareigis, “Equivalence Relations on Finite Dynamical Systems”, Advances in Applied Mathematics 26 (2001), 237–251. Figure 1, p. 250, shows a collection of state spaces for two Boolean variables. The source omits self-loops at fixed points.
